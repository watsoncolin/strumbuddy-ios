Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 21, 2026

Overview

StrumBuddy ("the App") is a guitar-learning app developed by Colin Watson. StrumBuddy is built to be private by design: it does not require an account, and it does not collect, store, or transmit your personal information.

Data We Collect

None. StrumBuddy does not collect personal data. There is no sign-up, no analytics, and no advertising. The App works fully offline and does not send any data to us or to third parties.

Microphone

StrumBuddy uses your device's microphone to listen to your guitar so it can tune, follow along, and tell you how cleanly you're playing. This audio is processed in real time on your device only. It is never recorded, saved, or transmitted, and it never leaves your device.

Your Practice Data

Your progress, streak, and skill history are stored locally on your device so the coach can track your improvement. This information stays on your device and is removed if you delete the App.

Data We Do Not Collect

  • Personal identification information (name, email, phone number)
  • Location data
  • Contacts or address book information
  • Photos or camera access
  • Recordings of your microphone audio
  • Usage analytics or advertising identifiers
  • Payment or financial information

Third-Party Services

StrumBuddy does not use any third-party analytics, advertising, or data-collection services.

Children's Privacy

StrumBuddy is suitable for all ages and does not collect data from anyone, including children.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a revised date.
