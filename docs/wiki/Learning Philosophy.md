# Learning Philosophy

The first three months are a **retention problem, not a teaching problem**
(~90% of beginners quit). Teaching is in service of habit. Everything is judged by:
*does it get someone from zero to consistent practice?* See [[Vision and Strategy]].

## Why beginners quit guitar
A brutal effort-to-reward curve early: sore fingertips, buzzing chords, slow
changes, a big gap between what they play and "real" playing — and no sense they're
improving. People quit not because it's hard but because it feels **futile and
shapeless**. The design must make progress *felt* and effort *small*.

## Teaching principles (the science)
- **Deliberate practice** (Ericsson) — focused effort on your specific weakness,
  just beyond current ability, with immediate feedback → our coach + [[Cleanliness Scoring]].
- **Zone of proximal development** (Vygotsky) — hard enough to stretch, easy enough
  to win → "weakest-but-ready" selection.
- **Mastery learning** (Bloom) — advance on demonstrated competence, defined as
  *reliable, not perfect* → our consistency criterion ([[The Coach]]).
- **Spaced repetition & retrieval** — resurface decaying skills → FSRS decay.
- **Flow** — clear goals + immediate feedback + matched challenge.

We're well-aligned on teaching. The **gap is the habit/behavior layer**.

## Behavior design (the retention half)
- **Make the first commitment tiny** (Fogg; the 2-minute rule). Lower activation energy.
- **Guarantee an early win** — session one must end in a celebrated real success.
- **A short daily ritual**, not long rare sessions — consistency > volume.
- **Streaks + loss aversion** (Duolingo) — paired with **forgiveness** (streak
  freezes, "welcome back"), because a spiraling lapse is the real churn moment.
- **Visible, earned progress** — the ladder, the radar, levels.
- **Intrinsic motivation = real music** — the carrot that beats any badge (songs,
  even 2-chord ones; full payoff is BYO-song in v2, see [[Roadmap]]).
- **Identity** — help them see themselves as *someone who plays guitar*.

## Proven app strategies
- **Duolingo** — bite-sized daily lessons, adjustable goal, streaks + freezes,
  instant feedback, spaced review. The habit engine *is* the product.
- **Couch-to-5K** — the model for 0→consistent on a hard physical skill: gentle,
  gradual, time-bounded, clear finish line. The shape the [[Structured Path]] should take.
- **Anki** — offload "what should I do today" to the algorithm; just show up.
- **Chess.com** — a tiny daily ritual (one puzzle) + an always-moving rating.
- **Yousician** — feedback + songs work, but it's knocked as *mechanical* — the gap
  our explainable cleanliness coaching exploits.

## Implications — the arc from 0 → habit
1. **First 5 minutes → a guaranteed win** (tune, play one clean chord, celebrate);
   also seeds the coach. (Onboarding)
2. **A daily session** — coach-picked, short, ends on a success. *The keystone.* See
   [[Daily Practice Loop]].
3. **Streaks + gentle lapse recovery.**
4. **Structured path as a Couch-to-5K ladder** — gradual, finish-lined, progress visible.
5. **A real song ASAP** — the motivation payoff.

**Priority reorder:** the next build is the *habit engine* (daily loop + first-win
onboarding), not more teaching — that's what the retention thesis rests on.
